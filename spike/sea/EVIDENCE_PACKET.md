Review limits and risks: (1) T2 writes the worker asset to `NMZP_DATA/sea-audit-runtime-worker.cjs` and loads it from disk on every spawn: a write-then-execute path in a user-writable directory. Production must verify an embedded hash before load or use `Worker(source, {eval:true})`; not accepted as-is. (2) Source isolation uses hide-files fallback, not a checkout rename (EBUSY); no file-access tracing. (3) Three other workers (agent-discovery, snapshot-status, probe-mailbox) untransformed; their SEA paths are unproven. (4) Authenticode shows only self-signed chain status; Ed25519 is the only mutation proof; neither is production trust. (5) Linux and first-after-reboot NOT_RUN. (6) The defaultSpawn transform is a source-text regex that refuses on mismatch; any runtime.ts edit breaks the build by design.
Verdict (coordinator, WP-10 scope): SEA on Windows ARM64 is FEASIBLE for cli/serve/hook/audit-worker paths; D (SEA) remains the preferred packaging direction, D-prime not needed on this evidence. Linux proof and the three untransformed workers are carried to WP-11+ as open items. Not a release/architecture freeze.
